Service policies

Questions, answered clearly.

How does the 2-inch trigger policy work?

Service begins when accumulation reaches 2 inches at our Hanover dispatch center. Crews monitor local conditions and prepare for the event.

Do I need to call when it snows?

Scheduled clients are serviced automatically when their plan is activated. If you need urgent access, call us directly.

How do I know the job was completed?

Ask about the confirmation and photo-documentation options available for your service plan.

A clear winter plan

From service selection to verified completion.

Set up your service once, then know exactly what happens each time snow falls. We keep the process straightforward, from your agreement to the final service photo.

  1. 01

    Select your service

    Choose residential, commercial, or emergency snow service and share the access details that help us plan your route.

  2. 02

    Sign your contract

    Review and sign your seasonal or service agreement so the scope, access expectations, and contact details are confirmed.

  3. 03

    Send your e-Transfer

    Make payment by Interac e-Transfer to snowpros@contractor.net after your agreement is approved.

  4. 04

    Receive verified service

    Service begins at 2 inches of accumulation during every snowfall, with photo verification provided for every completed job.
